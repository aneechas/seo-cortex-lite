# Competitive Analysis Procedure

## Purpose

Step-by-step procedure for identifying and analysing SEO competitors based on SERP evidence (never assumptions), conducting content and backlink gap analysis, and benchmarking technical SEO performance.

## Prerequisites

- Target domain URL
- 10-20 target keywords representing core business topics
- Target geography
- Industry context
- Backlink data access (for link gap analysis)

## Procedure

### Step 1: SERP-Based Competitor Identification

**Actions — competitors are discovered, never assumed:**
- Search each of the 10-20 target keywords
- Record every domain appearing in positions 1-10
- Count appearances across all target keywords per domain
- Calculate SERP overlap percentage: (keywords both rank for / total keywords) × 100
- Rank domains by overlap percentage
- Filter to top 5-8 true SERP competitors (highest overlap)
- Distinguish between:
  - **Direct competitors**: Same business type, similar offerings
  - **Content competitors**: Different business but competing for same keywords
  - **Aggregators/platforms**: Directories, review sites, marketplaces
- Note: Competitors you assume are rivals may not be SERP competitors

**Output:** Ranked competitor list with overlap scores and classification

### Step 2: Content Gap Analysis

**Actions:**
- For each SERP competitor, gather their ranking keywords
- Identify keywords where competitors rank but we don't:
  - Filter by minimum volume threshold (e.g. 100+ monthly searches)
  - Classify by search intent
  - Group into topic clusters
- Identify keywords where competitors rank higher:
  - Our position vs their position
  - Content quality comparison for those keywords
- Assess content format gaps:
  - Do competitors have content types we lack? (videos, tools, calculators, glossaries)
  - Do they cover topics at greater depth?
- Map gaps to business value (not all gaps are worth pursuing)

**Output:** Content gap matrix with volume, intent, and priority assessment

### Step 3: Backlink Gap Analysis

**Actions:**
- Gather referring domain data for target domain and top 5 competitors
- Identify domains linking to competitors but not to us:
  - Filter by domain authority/rating (focus on quality, not quantity)
  - Assess topical relevance of linking domains
  - Classify link type (editorial, resource, directory, guest post)
- Calculate link gap severity:
  - Total referring domain gap vs each competitor
  - Quality-weighted gap (DR-weighted count)
  - Unique linkers (domains linking to 2+ competitors but not us)
- Identify easiest acquisition opportunities:
  - Resource pages that already link to multiple competitors
  - Industry directories or associations
  - Unlinked brand mentions of our domain

**Output:** Link gap table with acquisition difficulty and priority

### Step 4: Technical SEO Benchmarking

**Actions:**
- For each top competitor, assess:
  - Core Web Vitals scores (PageSpeed Insights)
  - Mobile friendliness
  - Structured data implementation (types and coverage)
  - Site speed (TTFB, page load time)
  - HTTPS and security headers
  - Sitemap presence and quality
- Compare against our own scores
- Identify technical advantages competitors have
- Note technical weaknesses competitors have (opportunities)

**Output:** Technical comparison matrix with scores per dimension

### Step 5: SERP Feature Analysis

**Actions:**
- For each target keyword, document SERP features present:
  - Featured Snippets (who owns them, what format)
  - People Also Ask (recurring questions)
  - AI Overviews (who gets cited)
  - Knowledge Panels
  - Video carousels (who appears)
  - Image packs
  - Local packs (if applicable)
  - Site links, FAQs in SERPs
- Calculate our SERP feature presence vs competitors
- Identify feature opportunities (features present but we're not in them)

**Output:** SERP feature ownership matrix, opportunity list

### Step 6: Strategic Synthesis

**Actions:**
- Summarise each competitor's strategy (content-led, technical, link-driven)
- Identify our competitive advantages (what we do better)
- Identify our competitive disadvantages (where we trail)
- Recommend strategic response:
  - **Match**: Areas where we need to reach parity
  - **Differentiate**: Areas where we can take a unique angle
  - **Leapfrog**: Areas where competitor weakness + our effort = overtake
  - **Ignore**: Areas not worth competing (too costly, low business value)
- Prioritise recommendations by business impact and achievability

**Output:** Strategic recommendations with competitive positioning

## Final Output

Compile into a Competitive Intelligence Report following the market-researcher output format. Include: SERP competitor list, content gaps, link gaps, technical benchmarks, SERP feature analysis, and strategic recommendations.
